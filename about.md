  <p>i yearn for knowledge, yet im very far from my goal. i seek symbolism to express my inner state which is ultimately unexplainable and this paradox has put me under a lot of stress. countless attempts, constant trial and error to capture the essence of self, wandering in search of a way to achieve it, and in the end i'm left with a vibrant feeling of emptiness. definitely not trying to play the victim or anything, i'm just trying to figure out why i am attracted to the "why" so much, as if i've been assigned the role of someone responsible for connecting the dots that reveal the "process", conscious enough to fathom the insanity of it, but stupid enough to hunt it, until it reveals itself. The sacred geometry of gnosis is my second nature, and knowing that i know nothing, makes the weightlessness of both my nous and psyche recede. i'm here to fulfill a purpose im yet unaware of, or to experience the symbolism i paint. i'm recursive unto myself, trapped in a cycle of words. I'm tired of assigning labels that limit the ontological core of things, yet i still fall victim to the propaganda.</p>
